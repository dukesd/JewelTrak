Our Story

Software That Speaks Your Language

JewelTrak was created out of a simple observation: jewelers, diamond dealers, and watch professionals were forced to use generic business software that didn't understand their world.

We set out to build something different — a cloud-based platform designed from the ground up for the unique workflows of the jewelry trade. From tracking loose diamonds by the 4Cs to managing consignment inventory and generating professional appraisals, every feature in JewelTrak was built because a real jeweler needed it.

Today, JewelTrak serves retailers, wholesalers, and dealers who want a modern, reliable system they can access from anywhere — the showroom floor, a trade show, or the comfort of home.
